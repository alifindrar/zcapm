## Entry rule

Place a limit buy at **limit** (the 2026-10-05 close minus 0 x ATR(14), rounded down to the IDX tick) for 3 sessions. Whatever is still unfilled after session 3, buy at market at that session's close. Do not skip the name: missing a pick costs more than a few ticks.

Over 277 historical entries this rule filled 93% of the time and cost +0.04% on average against simply buying at the first open, so it mainly guards against paying up on a gap. Deeper limits (k = 0.5) save 1.1% on a typical fill but fill only 57% of the time, and the names that run away cost +4.7% when bought later, so on average waiting below the market does not pay.
